Outdoor Survival Basics

Good preparation can help make an outdoor trip safer. Here are some basic things to remember before going hiking or camping.

Before You Leave

Check the weather and learn about the area before your trip. Know your route and how long you expect to be outside.

Tell someone where you are going and when you expect to return.

Basic Items

Bring important items that can help during unexpected situations.

  • Water
  • Food
  • Map
  • Compass
  • Flashlight
  • First-aid supplies
  • Extra clothing
  • Sun protection

If You Get Lost

Stay calm and think before moving. Moving without knowing your location can make the situation more difficult.

Use your map, compass, phone, or other available tools to understand your location.

Weather Awareness

Weather can change quickly outdoors. Check the forecast before leaving and pay attention to changing conditions.

Carry clothing that is appropriate for the expected conditions.

Emergency Communication

A charged phone can be useful, but cell service may not be available everywhere. Know how you will communicate during an emergency before starting your trip.

Remember

Preparation is one of the most important parts of outdoor safety. Learn about the location, weather, and possible hazards before leaving.
