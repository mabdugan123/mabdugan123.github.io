Star Fire

A star fire places several pieces of wood around a central burning area.

What It Is

Several logs are placed on the ground with one end of each log pointing toward the center. From above, the arrangement looks like a star.

When to Use It

The arrangement allows wood to be moved toward the center as the ends burn.

How It Works

A small fire is started in the center. The ends of the larger pieces of wood are positioned near the flame and can be moved inward carefully as they burn.

Materials Needed

  • Tinder
  • Kindling
  • Several longer pieces of firewood
  • Safe ignition source

Safety Note

Keep the fire within an established fire area when possible. Never leave it unattended and completely extinguish it before leaving.

Source

Fire safety information: National Park Service - Campfires
