Trail Markers

Trail markers help hikers identify and follow established trails.

What It Is

Trail markers are signs, symbols, posts, or painted markings placed along established trails to help people follow a route.

When to Use It

Pay attention to trail markers when hiking on marked trails, especially near turns, intersections, and changes in the route.

How It Works

Hikers follow the official markings for their selected trail. A map can also help confirm the route and important locations.

Materials Needed

  • Trail map
  • Knowledge of the planned route
  • Compass or navigation tool

Safety Note

Do not create or change official trail markers. Stay on established trails when required and follow signs from park or land officials.

Source
