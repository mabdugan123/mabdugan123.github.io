Collecting Rainwater

Rainwater can be collected using a clean surface and container.

What It Is

Rainwater collection means catching rain and directing it into a container for later use.

When to Use It

It may provide an additional source of water when rain is available and normal water supplies are limited.

How It Works

A clean tarp or similar surface can direct falling rain toward a clean container. The collected water should be protected from additional contamination.

Materials Needed

  • Clean container
  • Clean tarp or collection surface
  • Cord if needed

Safety Note

Rainwater is not automatically safe to drink. It can collect germs and chemicals from the air or collection surface. Treat water when necessary before drinking it.

Source
